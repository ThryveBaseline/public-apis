- [3rd/high] Core premise of Fair Value / Fair Pricing Theory: with no new information, price reverts to a 'fair value' reference. [source](https://fxreplay.com/strategies/jj-simons-fair-value-theory-nq-strategy) (2026-04-29)
  > when no new information is available for the market to price in, price is likely to revert toward a reference point known as 'fair value'
- [3rd/high] AndrewFXTD's wording of the same premise (closest to JJ's phrasing). [source](https://www.tradingview.com/script/jfW4Vilk/) (2026)
  > If there is no 'new' information for the market to price in, it is most likely going to revert back to 'fair value.'
- [3rd/high] Two fair-value anchors for NQ: the 9:30 AM open price and the 2:00 PM NY afternoon price. [source](https://fxreplay.com/strategies/jj-simons-fair-value-theory-nq-strategy) (2026-04-29)
  > JJ has identified two price levels that consistently act as fair value: the 9:30 AM market open price and the 2:00 PM NY afternoon price
- [JJ/high] JJ's own framing (per transcript summary): fair price is the pre-open candle; the open is an 'unfair' institutional-volume move away from it. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/strategy-behind-1-6m-prop-firm-payouts/) (2026)
  > trading a continuation in the direction of the opening push and a reversion trade back toward the pre-open 'fair price' candle
- [3rd/medium] Third-party backtester's restatement of JJ's rationale for the opening edge. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/jj-simons-strategy-backtest-365-days/) (2026)
  > The idea is that the New York open creates an unfair move, a flood of institutional volume pushing price away from what he calls the fair price. And the fair price is just the price of the candle right before the open, the pre-open price, before all that institutional volume hits.
- [3rd/medium] Backtester's list of what makes the opening move 'unfair' (mispriced). [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/jj-simons-strategy-backtest-365-days/) (2026)
