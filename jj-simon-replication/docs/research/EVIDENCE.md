## Facts

### When to stop trading: daily/weekly limits, psychology, losing weeks

- [JJ/high] Hard time cutoff: he stops the morning session at 11:00 ET and does not re-enter even if the reversion happens afterwards. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/made-105700-3-weeks-day-trading-nq-futures/) (2026)
  > Unfortunately, I end trading at 11:00 and after 11:00, it reverted all the way back up here. ... 'Done for this day' at 11:00 a.m.
- [3rd/high] Sessions are fixed windows: 09:30-11:00 and 14:00-15:00 NY; first 10-15 minutes continuation, then mean reversion toward the 09:30 (or 14:00) price. [source](https://fxreplay.com/strategies/jj-simons-fair-value-theory-nq-strategy) (2026)
  > traded exclusively on the 1-minute chart during two New York sessions: 9:30-11:00 AM and 2:00-3:00 PM ... In the first 10-15 minutes, traders look for continuation moves ... For the remainder of the window, the focus shifts to mean-reversion setups
- [JJ/high] Per-account stop: on evals he goes max risk and quits the eval after exactly two winning trades (target hit) or treats a loss as a cheap eval and moves on. [source](https://jjwebinar.com/) (2026)
  > He goes maximum risk and clears the whole target in exactly two trades and then moves on. ... When JJ Simon loses a trade, it's described as a cheap eval and he moves on.
- [JJ/high] Funded accounts: risk drops sharply once funded ('aggressive to get funded, conservative to stay funded'). [source](https://jjwebinar.com/) (2026)
  > The moment he is funded, he flips gears with risk coming right down
- [JJ/high] Daily routine stop: one trade per account per day, one account at a time, so each account's exposure per day is a single ~1R loss or 1.5R win. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/strategy-behind-1-6m-prop-firm-payouts/) (2026)
  > He recommends trading one account at a time and one trade per account per day as the optimal way to trade prop firms.
- [JJ/high] No cap on total trades across accounts; he rejects the overtrading framing and trades ~10/day in the NY session. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/strategy-behind-1-6m-prop-firm-payouts/) (2026)
  > there's no maximum amount of trades ... aggressive trading with around 10 trades per day during the New York session
- [JJ/medium] Sizing is set around the daily loss limit and trailing drawdown so firm rules do not knock accounts out. [source](https://jjwebinar.com/) (2026)
  > sizing around the daily loss limit and trailing drawdown so the rules stop taking traders out
- [JJ/high] Losing weeks still produce payouts because risk is optimized per account (contracts and stop adjusted to volatility/candle size). [source](https://jjwebinar.com/) (2026)
  > JJ Simon adjusts contract size and stop loss based on candle size and market volatility to optimize risk management, ensuring payouts even during losing weeks ... even red weeks still grant him payouts
- [JJ/high] Concrete losing-week example: 'not my best week' still yielded $18,000 in payouts. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/made-105700-3-weeks-day-trading-nq-futures/) (2026)
  > this week was not my best week, but I was still able to take $18,000 worth of payouts
- [JJ/medium] Consistency over home runs; optimal risk management is what makes payouts possible in losing weeks. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/jj-simons-strategy-backtest-365-days/) (2026)
  > JJ emphasizes the importance of consistency over hitting home runs in prop firm trading, using optimal risk management to ensure payouts even during losing weeks
- [3rd/high] Fixed mechanics remove discretion: TP always 1.5R, no management, no partials; trade skipped if MSB/BOS + displacement conditions are not met. [source](https://fxreplay.com/strategies/jj-simons-fair-value-theory-nq-strategy) (2026)
  > The take profit is always set at 1.5R, with no trade management and no partial exits. ... if these exact conditions are not met, the trade is skipped
- [JJ/high] Risk per trade ~$1,000 via ATR tiers: ATR>20 -> 50pt SL/75pt TP (1 contract); ATR 7-20 -> 25/37.5 (2 contracts); ATR<7 -> 16.5/24.75 (3 contracts). He most often uses 25/37.5. [source](https://jjwebinar.com/) (2026)
  > He most often uses 25 point SL and 37.5 point TP for 1.5R, but in more volatile times he uses 50 point SL and 75 point TP. These SL distances with 1, 2, or 3 NQ contracts equate to $1k risk per trade.
- [JJ/high] His own statistics sample: 80 trades over 8 days (10/day), 57.5% win rate at 1.5R. [source](https://sozai.app/transcript/strategy-behind-1-6m-prop-firm-payouts/) (2026)
  > 80 trades over 8 days (10 trades per day) with a 57.5% win rate on 1.5 risk to reward
- [JJ/high] Eval pass math he uses: at 50% win rate, two wins in a row = 1 in 4, so ~1 in 4 evals funds; he 'just plays the numbers'. [source](https://jjwebinar.com/) (2026)
  > With a 50% win rate, two losses in a row occurs one in four times, so he knows roughly one in every four evals gets funded, and he just plays the numbers.
- [JJ/high] He calls trading psychology 'fake'; the mini-course lesson 4 is 'The Lie About Psychology - Why Trading Psychology Is Fake'. His mentorship nonetheless has a module 'The Killer In Trading: Emotions & Overtrading'. [source](https://jj.jjsimontrades.com/mini-course) (2026)
  > Lesson 04 'The Lie About Psychology' ... 'Why Trading Psychology Is Fake' ... 'JJ Simon 2026 Mentorship: The Killer In Trading Emotions & Overtrading Sample Video'
- [JJ/medium] Mentorship content covers funded-account consistency, trade recaps and a risk-management dashboard (review tooling). [source](https://whop.com/jj-s-mentorship-ba4a/) (2026)
  > focused on NQ trading, execution structure, risk management, funded-account consistency, trade recaps, and trading psychology ... entry model, trade recaps, and a risk management dashboard

  > breaks down the statistics, risk models and account strategies behind his prop firm success, from calculating optimal risk and R:R to managing 45+ accounts and knowing exactly when to stop trading

  > how to strategically adjust your risk on prop firm evaluation and funded accounts to maximize success
