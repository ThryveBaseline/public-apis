## Contradictions

- Account count: 20–30 (his X posts) vs 40 (video attribution) vs 45+ (Chart Fanatics blurb). Likely grew over time; no per-firm breakdown found anywhere.
- Execution: he says the optimal way is one account at a time, one trade per account per day, and not to copy trade until ~$20k/month; yet third-party summaries (fttuts, TikTok) describe copying one trade across dozens of accounts, and jjwebinar says he 'cycles one trade across multiple funded accounts'. Probably: evals traded individually, funded accounts copied.
- Trades per day: 'about 10 trades per day' (sozai $1.6M; his own 80 trades/8 days) vs '30 times/day' (Chart Fanatics title) vs 10–30 (task brief). 'One trade per account per day' × many accounts reconciles these.
- Payout totals rise across sources ($1.2M→$2M+) and 'verified' is self-asserted; E8 certificate is the only firm-issued proof surfaced.
- Stop sizes are quoted as 'ticks' in youtubesummary/one search summary but as 'points' (50/25/16.5) on fxreplay; points ($20/pt NQ) matches the ~$1,000 risk figure.
- Tradeify is named on jjwebinar as a payout source but did not appear in the $1.6M per-firm breakdown excerpt.
