### From the primary corpus: winning days, payout cadence and timing

**1. Rules in his own words**

*1.1 The payout rules are the floor; the balance is the ceiling.* He describes the funded account as a container whose contents can only be withdrawn in the slices the payout rules allow: ("Your funded account balance is the ceiling, the payout rules are the floor. The problem is most traders get caught in between those." — JJ, 0Ua-ffltO0I ~4:33, L229); ("which is the entire reason why the money that you see in your sim funded account can never fully be withdrawn as a payout, and that is why rules are what actually matter on prop firms more than the strategy." — JJ, 0Ua-ffltO0I ~1:56, L123). The gates he lists: ("Once you get to the funded account, well, now you have to make another X amount of dollars, you have to achieve a buffer, you have to meet consistency, you have to meet their winning day rule, and then you can only take out 50% of what you make." — JJ, 0Ua-ffltO0I ~2:11, L133); ("Some have minimum balance requirements, minimum days of earnings, withdrawal limits, and consistency rules that reset your cycle if you don't meet them." — JJ, S8fSNbPFuLA ~8:54, L374). The motive he attributes to the firms: ("That is why they put so many rules on the eval, so many rules on the funded account, is because they want to reduce your expected value as much as possible, so they can keep making as much money as possible" — JJ, 0Ua-ffltO0I ~1:49, L118). The funded account, not the evaluation, is where repeat payouts come from: ("The eval ends completely the instant you pass it. On a funded account though, it doesn't end until you breach a rule. You can take multiple payouts from a funded account. That should be your goal." — JJ, 0Ua-ffltO0I ~4:14, L216).

*1.2 The standard cycle: five winning days of $150, then half the balance up to a cap.* He states this for Topstep, for Lucid and for "the top firms" generally: ("And their payout rules are literally just winning days. So, five winning days $150 and you can take out half of your balance up to 5k." — JJ, 5RzMu2B2E_0 ~18:05, L632) (Topstep 150K); ("On the funded account, to get the payout, you need 5 days of $150, and you can withdraw 50% of your profit up to $2,000." — JJ, wsKx7L9EJys ~1:21, L65) (Lucid 50K Flex); ("Withdrawal requirements at most funded accounts, such as Topstep, Lucid, Fritify, any of the top firms, require five winning days of $150." — JJ, 0uAQUHEB_L8 ~6:17, L256; medium confidence). The $150 is a threshold: ("You have to realize that you need $150 to get a winning day. If you get less, it doesn't count. If you get more, it does count. But, remember that you need five of them to get to the payout." — JJ, wsKx7L9EJys ~8:43, L293). Winning days accumulate and are never lost on down days: ("At this point, you'll have five winning days, and then you can withdraw half of the balance, which is $1,500." — JJ, 0uAQUHEB_L8 ~8:11, L324); for a coin-flip trader ("it will take you 10 days on average to get five winning days" — JJ, 0uAQUHEB_L8 ~8:47, L345). The two phases have different objectives: ("Evaluation, only earn $3,000. Funded account to win any amount of money, five winning days and then withdraw the payment" — JJ, S8fSNbPFuLA ~6:06, L266). Cadence at most firms: ("most of the time you can't withdraw more than once per week, obviously depending on the firm rules, sometimes you can, but most firms are going to let you get one payout every five winning days." — JJ, 0Ua-ffltO0I ~5:29, L267); In the weekly videos the rule produces deliberately tiny trades taken only to log a day: ("I was just trying to qualify my account towards payout, so I needed like a $200 win, a $400 win, and a $150 win, so I was just like tiny little scalp." — JJ, EyR3i2GxwyA ~15:31, L541); and targets set by what the account still needs: ("a top tick, take profit just cuz my account needed to pay. So that's just how much it went for." — JJ, PN1UKQMPb5M ~32:12, L989); ("I needed, I don't know, 5,100, that amount of profit to get the maximum payout." — JJ, pwO9IAu7FUQ ~6:45, L251)..

**4. Contradictions**

Within his statements: (a) he says of the Lucid 50K Flex ("you can withdraw 50% of your profit up to $2,000." — JJ, wsKx7L9EJys ~1:21, L65) and of Topstep ("you can take out half of your balance up to 5k." — JJ, 5RzMu2B2E_0 ~18:05, L632); on a sim account whose balance is start plus profit these are different bases, and the 22-account model uses "50% of your balance" (0uAQUHEB_L8 L369) while the $3,000-to-$1,500 step in wsKx7L9EJys uses half the profit; the corpus does not settle which base a given firm uses. (b) ("most firms are going to let you get one payout every five winning days." — JJ, 0Ua-ffltO0I ~5:29, L267) (Sept 2026) sits beside firms he says pay daily (FundedNext, Vest, Tradeify Select Daily, Take Profit Trader live); the generic statement is about "most", so this is scope, not contradiction. With the dossier: Section 9's Topstep row gives "5 winning days of $150+, $125 min, $3,000-$4,000 per request" without the half-balance limit he states and without the $5,000 he gives for the 150K. `PropAccount.month_end` pays once a month and counts every traded day toward `payout_min_days`; he describes one payout per five winning days of at least $150, roughly every 10 trading days for a coin-flip trader, at most weekly at most firms and daily at some. Section 5 describes the simulator's "monthly cadence" as if it were a firm rule; no firm rule he states is monthly except GoFunded's two windows.
