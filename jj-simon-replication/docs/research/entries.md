## Contradictions

- Continuation window: JJ's own video says first 5 minutes; FX Replay and AndrewFXTD codify 10-15 minutes; FX Replay's backtest additionally skips the first 3 minutes.
- Grading vs behaviour: JJ says take only A+ (break of structure) setups, but also says on prop firms he takes 'literally any displacement entry seen towards fair price' (grade A).
- Entry location: AndrewFXTD/FX Replay enter at market on the displacement close; joetroyer's continuation setup waits for a retrace to the fair line.
- Trades per day: ~10 (JJ main video) vs 20 (Titans of Tomorrow chapter) vs 30 (Chart Fanatics title).
- Fair value anchor definition: '9:30 open price' (FX Replay) vs 'candle right before the open / pre-open candle' (JJ and 365-day backtester) vs joetroyer's configurable sources (candle before volume spike, 9:29 line, VWAP, prev-day close).
