## Facts

1. **Hard time cutoff: he stops the morning session at 11:00 ET and does not re-enter even if the reversion happens afterwards.**  
   > "Unfortunately, I end trading at 11:00 and after 11:00, it reverted all the way back up here. ... 'Done for this day' at 11:00 a.m."  
   — [Road to $1M Ep.2 / $1.6M transcript (sozai.app)](https://sozai.app/transcript/made-105700-3-weeks-day-trading-nq-futures/) · transcript-summary of JJ video · JJ's own words/source · 2026 · confidence high
2. **Sessions are fixed windows: 09:30–11:00 and 14:00–15:00 NY; first 10–15 minutes continuation, then mean reversion toward the 09:30 (or 14:00) price.**  
   > "traded exclusively on the 1-minute chart during two New York sessions: 9:30-11:00 AM and 2:00-3:00 PM ... In the first 10-15 minutes, traders look for continuation moves ... For the remainder of the window, the focus shifts to mean-reversion setups"  
   — [JJ Simon's Fair Value Theory Strategy (fxreplay.com)](https://fxreplay.com/strategies/jj-simons-fair-value-theory-nq-strategy) · third-party strategy write-up · third party · 2026 · confidence high
3. **Per-account stop: on evals he goes max risk and quits the eval after exactly two winning trades (target hit) or treats a loss as a cheap eval and moves on.**  
   > "He goes maximum risk and clears the whole target in exactly two trades and then moves on. ... When JJ Simon loses a trade, it's described as a cheap eval and he moves on."  
   — [JJ Simon — Free Live Training (jjwebinar.com)](https://jjwebinar.com/) · JJ sales/webinar page · JJ's own words/source · 2026 · confidence high
4. **Funded accounts: risk drops sharply once funded ('aggressive to get funded, conservative to stay funded').**  
   > "The moment he is funded, he flips gears with risk coming right down"  
   — [JJ Simon — Free Live Training (jjwebinar.com)](https://jjwebinar.com/) · JJ sales/webinar page · JJ's own words/source · 2026 · confidence high
5. **Daily routine stop: one trade per account per day, one account at a time — so each account's exposure per day is a single ~1R loss or 1.5R win.**  
   > "He recommends trading one account at a time and one trade per account per day as the optimal way to trade prop firms."  
   — [The Strategy Behind My $1.6M in Prop Firm Payouts (sozai.app)](https://sozai.app/transcript/strategy-behind-1-6m-prop-firm-payouts/) · transcript-summary of JJ video · JJ's own words/source · 2026 · confidence high
6. **No cap on total trades across accounts; he rejects the overtrading framing and trades ~10/day in the NY session.**

## Contradictions

- 'Psychology is fake' (mini-course lesson 4) vs a paid mentorship module titled 'The Killer In Trading: Emotions & Overtrading'.
- 'No maximum amount of trades' / 30 trades a day vs 'one trade per account per day is optimal' — reconciled only if the cap is per account, not per trader.
- Third parties describe eval posture as 'aggressive' (max risk, 2 trades) but a transcript also says a conservative eval uses 2–4 trades; the two co-exist as options.
- Chart Fanatics promises 'knowing exactly when to stop trading' from statistics, but no indexed source states a dollar/R/loss-count daily stop; the only hard stops found are the 11:00 clock and per-account one-trade/two-trade rules.
