## Contradictions

- Risk vs eval drawdown: $1,000 per trade on a $2,000-drawdown eval (50% of drawdown per trade) sits oddly with JJ's 'higher pass rate' claim; a third party notes two losses exhaust the drawdown. JJ's own eval sizing may differ from the funded $1,000 figure.
- Point value: one search rendering of the AndrewFXTD description says 'one point = $1 on NQ' while FX Replay says $20/point; $20 is correct for NQ ($2 for MNQ).
- Target rendering: 37.5 points (codifiers) vs 38 points (JJ transcript summary) vs '76 tick profit' in one summary of the wide-stop variant (75 points expected).
- Cost-per-drawdown arithmetic: $4,500 / $750 = 6, yet the quoted figure is 16.6 (likely 16.6 cents per $1 of drawdown, i.e. $750/$4,500).
- Posture: 'aggressive to get funded, conservative to stay funded' vs 'take literally any displacement entry' on prop firms.
- Trades per day: 10 vs 20 vs 30 across JJ's video, Titans of Tomorrow and Chart Fanatics.
- Tweet date: the eval-vs-funded tweet is indexed as 2024-09-13 although the account shows 'joined January 2026'.
