## 7. Contradictions

1. **Funded Engineer.** Credited with ~$180,000 in the July 2026 video, but Funded Engineer (Dubai FX prop) filed for bankruptcy on 15 July 2024 after FPFX revoked its licence in Feb 2024, before JJ's trading career began. The firm name is probably misheard in the transcript (candidates: Funded Futures Network "FFN", "FFF", "Funding Futures" from his X firm list). Unresolved.
2. **Trades per day.** ~10/day in his own videos vs "30 Times/Day" in the Chart Fanatics title. Possibly 10 per session across 3-4 sessions.
3. **Per-firm totals sum to ~$1.15M** against a $1.6M headline, leaving ~$450k unitemized (Tradeify and others).
4. **Bios lag:** YouTube $2M+, TikTok $1.8M+, X $1.5M+ (older $1.3M), Instagram $1.5M at the same time.
5. **Income promise:** Instagram "$10K+/month" vs YouTube/TikTok "$30K+/month".
6. **365-day backtest** reported as 51.89%/PF 1.94/56.69% vs 49%/PF 1.7/55%.
7. **Copy trading:** JJ says one account at a time, occasionally two, no copier until ~$20k; a third-party summary says the method "teaches traders to use trade copiers across dozens of smaller funded accounts".
8. **Mentorship price:** $49/month tier vs reseller "original $6,500/$6,000".
9. **Acceleration:** $1.5M (~June 2026) to $2M+ (Sept 2026) implies ~$500k in ~3 months, not itemized anywhere found.
10. **Trading Nut podcast:** a snippet implied a "JJ" guest; could not be tied to JJ Simon. Unverified.
