# Assumptions made where the public record is silent

Every rule in `fpt/` traces either to a public statement by JJ Simon or to a
third-party codification of his method (fxreplay, TradingView indicators,
backtesters). The items below are places where those sources do not pin a
value down, so the code exposes a parameter and documents the default it
ships with. Each one is a dial you should test against real NQ data.

| 39 | The frozen evaluator sizes every trade at exactly its risk budget (R x $1,000 in a Topstep 50K evaluation, the "two-trade" sizing), and a sealed stop-out costs 1.02 R with slippage and commission, so two stop-outs cost $2,040 against the $2,000 drawdown and end the evaluation (on the frozen preset the $1,000 daily limit caps each at exactly $1,000 and the balance lands on the threshold, which also fails). Sized 0.98 the account fails on the third stop-out instead and needs a third win (two make 2.96 R). Every Topstep 50K rate and Topstep 100K's payout rates at the sealed sizing, the sealed report's included, sit on this edge (a Topstep 100K evaluation fails on two stop-outs at any size). Below the sealed sizing, and at it after a day that started with a win, the frozen account's soft daily limit is not usable: it credits a later trade on a day that has nearly reached the limit with a full win but a loss cut to the room left, which no real account allows. Research therefore shows the size sensitivity (1.00, 0.98, 0.95) and whole micro contracts (full stop-out strictly within the budget) on `topstep_50k_x` only, and the frozen preset at the sealed sizing only. Classified B (sizing convention, not his rule). | B3 verifications, 2026-10-07 |
| 40 | Topstep's Express Funded rules after a payout. With a payout the maximum loss limit moves to the starting balance and stays there. A payout after the first needs a positive net profit since the previous one; the first is exempt. The frozen account leaves the limit trailing and has neither rule. Topstep's advice is to wait until the limit has reached the starting balance before asking. Both policies run: "ask" (as the frozen walk-forward does, and gated against it) and "wait". Not modelled in B4: the call-up to a Live account. Topstep's risk team makes it at its discretion, typically between the trader's 3rd and 5th payout, and closes every Express Funded account; the combined balances move to the Live account, 20% available and 80% in reserve. Daily Live payouts unlock after 30 winning days there. B5 adds the call-up at the 3rd payout as a lower bound: every account closes, and the Live account counts for nothing. Source: the Topstep help centre, read through a search engine. Classified B (firm rules). | B4 (`research/lifetime.py`), 2026-10-07 |
