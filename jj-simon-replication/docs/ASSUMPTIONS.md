# Assumptions made where the public record is silent

Every rule in `fpt/` traces either to a public statement by JJ Simon or to a
third-party codification of his method (fxreplay, TradingView indicators,
backtesters). The items below are places where those sources do not pin a
value down, so the code exposes a parameter and documents the default it
ships with. Each one is a dial you should test against real NQ data.

| # | Parameter | Default | Why this default | Where to change it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fair value anchor | `open_0930` (open of the 09:30 ET 1-minute bar) | JJ: "the fair price is 95% of the time going to be the market open"; fxreplay uses "the 9:30 AM market open price"; the 365-day backtester "the candle right before the open"; krisskross18 the 09:29 candle open. The other 5% of cases are not described publicly. The 09:30 open and the 09:29 close are the same print to within a tick. `close_0929` and `vwap_0929_0930` are provided for comparison. | `StrategyConfig.anchor` |
| 2 | Continuation window length | 5 minutes (09:30-09:35) | JJ's own $1.6M video: continuation only in the first ~5 minutes, then reversion for the remaining ~85. fxreplay and AndrewFXTD codify "the first 10-15 minutes" and fxreplay's optimisation also skips the first 3 minutes. Test 09:35, 09:40 and 09:45. | `StrategyConfig.continuation_end` |
| 3 | Window end | 11:00 ET | "a 90-minute window" from the open. | `StrategyConfig.window_end` |
| 4 | Afternoon session | off (14:00-15:00 when enabled) | JJ's earlier videos describe a 14:00-15:00 NY session anchored to the 14:00 price; his Sept 2026 videos add 6PM and 8PM sessions that are not codified anywhere public yet. Enable with `pm_session=True`. | `StrategyConfig.pm_session`, `pm_start`, `pm_end` |
| 5 | Premium/discount band | 38 points, not required | The 38-point band is joetroyer's TradingView codification, not a number JJ has published. Off by default; `require_band_touch=True` reproduces the indicator's reversion gate. | `StrategyConfig.band_points`, `require_band_touch` |
| 6 | Displacement candle | his definition: body larger than the previous candle's body and a close beyond it (`displacement_mode="jj"`) | Primary corpus (KN7j6NXXAio ~6:35 L275; KHEQ5g55dQ4 ~5:22 L236; 0uAQUHEB_L8 ~17:32 L657). fxreplay's 20% counter-wick test plus a body-size filter remains available as `displacement_mode="wick"` with `wick_pct`, `min_body_atr`. | `displacement_mode`, `wick_pct`, `min_body_atr` |
| 7 | ATR definition | Wilder RMA, period 14, on 1-minute bars | The tiers (above 20 / 7-20 / below 7) are published; the ATR period is not. 14 is TradingView's default and the most likely number he reads off the chart. | `StrategyConfig.atr_period`, `indicators.atr(method=)` |
| 8 | Swing definition for BOS/MSB | a wick lower/higher than the one candle before and the one after (pivot 1/1), used once confirmed | His own description (KHEQ5g55dQ4 ~6:54 L283; DEkzU8gqX-o ~6:13 L241). fxreplay-style 3/3 pivots: `swing_left=3, swing_right=3`. | `swing_left`, `swing_right` |
| 9 | Structure lookback | 60 bars | A swing from 90 minutes ago is not "recent structure". | `structure_lookback` |
| 10 | Grade A entries | taken | JJ names A+ (break of structure), A (displacement) and B (avoid). Whether he takes grade A alone is not stated explicitly; the aggressive ~10 trades/day cadence suggests yes. `allow_grade_a=False` restricts to A+. | `allow_grade_a` |
| 11 | Entry fill | market at the open of the next bar, plus 0.25 points slippage | He enters on the displacement close; the first fill after a 1-minute close is the next bar's open. | `slippage_points` |
| 12 | Stop fill | stop price minus 0.25 points slippage; if a bar touches both stop and target, the stop is assumed to fill first | Conservative convention. | `strategy.py` exit logic |
| 13 | Commission | $2.50 per contract per side | Typical retail futures all-in rate on NQ; prop firms charge similar. | `commission_per_contract_side` |
| 14 | One position at a time | yes in the backtester | He layers: one account per successive structure break (PN1UKQMPb5M ~1:25 L72). The backtester trades one position per signal stream; `portfolio --routing round_robin` spreads signals across accounts. | not configurable |
| 15 | Flat at window end | off: an open position runs to its stop or target (closed at the last bar of the data) | JJ: "After 11:00 a.m., I am done trading as well, but I'll let a position play out if I'm still in one" (UVKVSWKFlvo ~7:49 L288). On = flatten at the window end. | `flat_at_window_end` |
| 16 | Max trades per day | 100 (no maximum when winning) | JJ: "there's no maximum amount of trades" (KN7j6NXXAio ~1:45 L101); his cap counts losing attempts (row 17). | `max_trades_per_day` |
| 17 | Stop-trading rule | three consecutive losses end the session (`max_consecutive_losses=3`, `stop_scope="session"`) | JJ: "if I'm trying to trade reversion and I lose three in a row, then I'm done" (KHEQ5g55dQ4 ~34:24 L1124; ~1:10:50 L2238); April 2026: a fourth attempt only for an A+ setup. `fpt.risk.daily_stop_from_stats` still gives the statistical R stop. | `max_consecutive_losses`, `stop_scope`, `daily_loss_stop_r` |
| 18 | Stop, target and size | 25-point stop, 38-point target ("3825"), contracts = risk_dollars / (stop x $20) with risk_dollars 500 (one NQ contract) | His bracket (0uAQUHEB_L8 ~17:02 L639, ~19:55 L742); one contract per $500 in every evaluation demonstration (KHEQ5g55dQ4 ~1:07:52 L2147); 50/75 on 150k accounts; funded accounts larger. fxreplay's ATR ladder: `stop_mode="atr_tier", size_mode="tier"`. | `stop_mode`, `stop_points`, `target_points`, `size_mode`, `risk_dollars`, `max_contracts` |
| 19 | Prop firm rule presets | per-preset `verified` flag: True = from the firm's help center (7 of 12), False = third-party placeholder | Firm rules change monthly. Confirm every number on the firm's site before trusting a simulation. | `fpt/propfirm.py` |
| 20 | Copy trading | every account gets identical signals and R outcomes | This is what a trade copier does; slippage differences across accounts are ignored. | `PortfolioConfig.copy_trading` |
| 21 | Opening-candle rule | on: if a session's opening candle body exceeds 25 points, the continuation trade off it uses the 50-point stop at half size | JJ: "In the opening candle, if it is greater than 25 points, you can reduce the size" (0uAQUHEB_L8 ~17:02 L639); measured on the body ("The candle body is 33 points", xTxrtFKlQfc ~1:57 L94); applied per session to the continuation trade. Legacy: `big_open_measure="range", big_open_scope="am"`. | `big_open_candle_points`, `big_open_measure`, `big_open_scope` |
| 22 | fxreplay time filters | off | fxreplay's filtered variant skips the first 3 minutes for continuations and takes reversions only until about 10:00 (reported 62% win rate, PF 2.46 vs 52-54% / 1.66-1.76 unfiltered). | 23 | Rolling fair value | on | JJ, standing rule: "I change fair price throughout the day based on most recent consolidation" (primary corpus, fair_value_sessions synthesis); the Titans of Tomorrow clip says the same. Off = the session anchor only. | `rolling_fair_value`, `consolidation_bars`, `consolidation_atr_mult` |
| 24 | Other sessions | off (`--all-sessions` turns them on) | His stated day: 08:30 news (one continuation of the news candle, three or four reversions to the pre-news price), 09:30, one longer trade from 11:00 into 14:00, then 14:00, 18:00 and 20:00 sessions, each "one continuation, three to four reversions". Session lengths for 18:00 and 20:00 are assumed (90 minutes). The 11:00-14:00 "longer trade" is not implemented. | `extra_sessions` |
| 25 | Swing invalidation | any close through a swing level consumes it, on every bar | Matches the Pine port (`ta.pivothigh` + `close > lastPH`). A+ is awarded only when the displacement candle itself is the first close through the most recent live swing. | not configurable |
| 26 | Band-touch extremes | measured from each session's own start | The joetroyer indicator measures the band from each session anchor; a morning spike no longer satisfies the afternoon gate. | not configurable |
| 27 | Stop scope | per session | First-hand (KHEQ5g55dQ4 ~34:24 L1124): three losses in a row end that session; `stop_scope="day"` makes the stops once per day. | `stop_scope` |
| 28 | Fixed target points | 38 (his bracket) | 0uAQUHEB_L8 ~17:02 L639. Funded accounts without a consistency rule: 100-point targets (KHEQ5g55dQ4 ~8:38 L336), `--target-points 100`. None = rr x stop. | `target_points` |
| 29 | Window-end exit | at the open of the first bar at or after the window end; a signal whose fill bar is that bar is skipped | Nothing that happens inside the 11:00 bar counts once the position is flat at its open. | `flat_at_window_end` |

| 30 | Continuation direction | the colour of the session's opening 1-minute candle | JJ (3L8xdh3oPm4 ~11:30 L412; PN1UKQMPb5M ~0:42 L51). `continuation_direction="side_of_fv"` uses the side of fair value instead. | `continuation_direction` |
| 31 | Continuation stall | the continuation phase ends early once two consecutive candles close against the opening direction | JJ: stop continuations once the opening move stalls (l6iq0ljhxIo ~23:56 L790; PN1UKQMPb5M ~14:08 L451). None = time only. | `continuation_stall_candles` |
| 32 | Reversion room rule | reversion only when the distance to fair value is at least 0.8 x the target | JJ: at most 20% of the target may lie beyond fair value (KhooqEQK9bA ~11:04 L471; KHEQ5g55dQ4 ~1:22:33 L2596); he refuses 38-point reversions with under about 30 points of room. | 33 | Payout mechanics | a day counts toward a payout only if it made at least $150; each request pays 50% of profit above the buffer, then the cap | JJ (wsKx7L9EJys ~8:43 L293, ~1:21 L65); per-firm overrides in `FIRM_PRESETS`. | `FirmRules.winning_day_min`, `FirmRules.payout_fraction` |
| 34 | Portfolio defaults | round-robin routing, 20 signals a day across all accounts, 22 trading days a month | His operation: one trade per account per day across 40-45 accounts at 20-30 trades a day (XhsbfEdJBAc ~5:23 L216; KHEQ5g55dQ4). | 35 | Win rate assumption | 0.42 at 1.5R (his own latest explicit figure); every simulated result is a scenario until `evaluate` measures it | "a realistic average win rate is about 41%" (0uAQUHEB_L8 ~18:22 L687); "42% at 1.5R" (4IGbxmKJ4BU ~0:56 L68). | 36 | His calculator | one payout per funded account, no profit split: $100 x 0.33 x 0.33 x $2,000 = $217.80 back | XhsbfEdJBAc ~0:49 L58, ~2:02 L100. Repeat payouts and splits are the separate lifetime model (`--funded-mode repeat`, `--profit-split`). | `his_calculator`, `HisStatsConfig` |
